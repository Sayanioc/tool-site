Why trust these calculators?

  • Standard formulas only – IEC 60751 for RTDs, NIST ITS-90 reference functions for thermocouples, IEC 60534-2-1 for control valves. The standard is cited on every page.
  • Tested – the math lives in small pure JavaScript modules with automated tests against published reference values (e.g. Pt100 at 100 °C = 138.5055 Ω, type K at 100 °C = 4.096 mV).
  • Private and fast – everything runs in your browser. No sign-up, no trackers, no ads, works offline once loaded.
  • Plant units – SI and °C by default, with kg/cm², mmH2O and other units used in Indian and international plants.
