Temperature Converter (°C, °F, K, °R)

Type in any box – the others update instantly. Switch to temperature difference to convert spans, errors and tolerances, where no offset is applied.

Convert

What this converter does

It converts temperatures between the Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin and Rankine scales. In difference mode it converts temperature intervals – a transmitter span, a calibration error or a tolerance – which scale by 1.8 between °C and °F with no 32° offset.

Formula

K  = °C + 273.15          °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
°R = K × 9/5 = °F + 459.67
Differences: Δ°C = ΔK,  Δ°F = Δ°R = 1.8 × Δ°C

Worked example

A transmitter is ranged 0–400 °C. In °F the range is 32–752 °F, but its span is 400 × 1.8 = 720 °F. A ±0.5 °C tolerance is ±0.9 °F, not ±32.9 °F.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the 32° offset to a span, error or tolerance – use difference mode.
  • Using 273 instead of 273.15 for kelvin (0.15 K error).
  • Writing "°K" – the kelvin has no degree sign.

Standard

BIPM SI Brochure (9th ed.) – kelvin and degree Celsius definitions; ITS-90 temperature scale; NIST SP 811 for °F and °R.
